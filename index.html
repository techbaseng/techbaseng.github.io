About Techbase Academy

Free STEM education
for everyone.

Techbase STEM Academy is a free learning platform built by Techbase Consultant Services in Ibadan, Nigeria. Our mission is to make high-quality STEM and coding education accessible to every student regardless of background or resources.

All six courses are completely free, hosted on GitHub Pages and open source under the MIT licence. The curriculum spans from absolute beginner (Scratch, HTML) to advanced data science, algorithms and AI — designed by Techbase Consultant services.

  • 6 complete courses · web, software and hardware
  • 356 lessons, projects and hands-on guides
  • All linked to official sources — Raspberry Pi, microbit.org
  • Suitable for ages 8 through adult
  • Used in schools and youth programmes across Ibadan
