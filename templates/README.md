# templates

Page layouts used by the build. `base.html` is the shared header, footer and search. `home.html`, `about.html`, `services.html`, `team.html`, `testimonials.html`, `portfolio*.html`, `blog.html`, `post.html`, `tools.html`, `faq.html`, `contact.html`, `careers.html` and `privacy.html` are company pages. `learn.html` (Academy home), `course.html` and `lesson.html` are the Academy. `login.html` and `account.html` are the account pages. `_macros.html` and `_sprite.html` hold shared pieces and icons. `theme_inline.js` prevents a flash of the wrong theme.
