Our company

Techbase Consultant Services.

At Techbase Consultant Services, our goal is to make you shine like a star. We provide consultant services to schools and private individuals in STEM education, curriculum and teaching aids, coding and robotics training, website and app development, AI tools for education, and tutor training. We are based in Ibadan, Nigeria, and work with clients across the country.

We believe you are your best accessory. That is why we offer a full-service experience: structured enough to be dependable, hands-on enough to move the needle, rather than generic off-the-shelf training with no follow-through.

Our mission is to deliver exceptional STEM coding and robotics education to learners across all levels, and to help individuals, schools and teams thrive through coding, robotics, digital skills and practical technology consulting.
