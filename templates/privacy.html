What we collect

  • Account details (only if you create an account): your name, email address and password, handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see your password. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name and email address from Google.
  • Learning data (only if you have an account): the lessons you finish, the lesson you opened last, and an optional learning goal you choose. This lets your progress follow you between devices.
  • Messages you send us: the contact and careers forms store what you type (name, email, phone, message, and for careers a CV link) so that we can reply.

What stays on your device

Your theme choice, your lesson progress (when you are not signed in) and a flag that remembers you were signed in are kept in your browser's local storage. This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies and uses no analytics.

Who handles it

  • Google Firebase provides sign-in and the database that stores account, progress and form data.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files.
  • Links to WhatsApp, social networks and partner sites leave this website. Their own privacy terms apply there.

Your choices

  • You can browse the whole website, including the free preview lessons, without an account.
  • Signed-in learners can change their name, and delete their account and saved progress at any time from the Account page.
  • To ask us to remove a message or application you sent, email {{ site.company.email }}.

Children

Learners under 13 should create an account only together with a parent, guardian or teacher.
