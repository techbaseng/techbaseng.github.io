Techbase Consultant Services was deeply honoured to be part of something truly historic in Akinyele LGA. We were proud to support this landmark initiative for the entire local community.

The maiden edition of the Akinyele Spelling Challenge 2026, organized by Ivory Educational Resources under the visionary leadership of Pastor Moore Olatunde, brought together over 50 talented primary school pupils from both public and private schools across the Akinyele Local Government Area. This inaugural academic competition, held at the Funduk Hall in Moniya, Ibadan, provided a prestigious platform for young learners to demonstrate their spelling proficiency and linguistic skills in a high-stakes environment. By gathering students from diverse educational backgrounds, the event successfully fostered a culture of excellence and healthy rivalry, reflecting Pastor Olatunde's deep-seated dedication to improving educational standards and empowering the next generation of leaders within the local community.

![A winner of the Akinyele Spelling Challenge 2026 holding her prize](photo:spelling-winner)

Following a display of exceptional talent and dedication, three remarkable young minds emerged as the competition's champions:

- 🥇 Olatunji Treasure — Akingbile Community (awarded the grand prize of ₦100,000)
- 🥈 Luqman Abdulsalam — Igbo-Oloyin Community (earning a second-place reward of ₦50,000)
- 🥉 Ejiro Bethel — Sagbe Community (securing the third-place prize of ₦30,000)

Our Founder and CEO, Babatunde Awoyemi, served as the Head of Program and Logistics for the event, where he was responsible for coordinating the full programme rundown. His leadership ensured a seamless flow of activities, beginning with the initial contestant check-in at 7:30 AM and concluding with the final prize presentation and official group photographs. By managing these logistics, he ensured the event met its objectives and professional standards. The proceedings were also widely recognized in the media, receiving coverage from both National Daily News and Online News Hub.

![Pupils seated and waiting at the Akinyele Spelling Challenge 2026](photo:spelling-audience)

Understanding the rules of language is the first step toward mastering the logic of algorithms. This is why Techbase remains committed to supporting every layer of a student's education, championing fundamental academic skills alongside digital innovation.

We are incredibly proud of Ivory Educational Resources for developing this vital grassroots platform, which empowers students through accessible learning.
