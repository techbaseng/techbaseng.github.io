The initiative transformed local centers into digital learning hubs, allowing first-time learners to explore computer science in supportive environments. This approach addressed the digital divide by providing access to technology and encouraging students to engage with STEM subjects during their break from school.

![Children working on laptops and electronics at a community STEM session](photo:community-robotics)

By taking STEM initiatives out of the traditional classroom and into accessible community spaces, Techbase delivered vital coding and technology education to children who otherwise would not have access to these critical resources. These localized programs provide hands-on learning opportunities and technical mentorship, effectively bridging the digital divide for underserved youth who are often overlooked by mainstream educational systems.

![Two children sharing a laptop in a community STEM session](photo:holiday-laptop)

By placing laptops directly in front of first-time learners during the SCHOOL holidays, the organisation transformed local centres into hubs of digital discovery.

![Children gathered around laptops at a community learning centre](photo:community-class)

This hands-on approach empowered students to explore computer science in a supportive environment, effectively bridging the digital divide and fostering curiosity during their break from school.
