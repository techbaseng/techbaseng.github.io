To commemorate International Day of the Girl 2021, Techbase Consultant Services partnered with the Initiative for Integrated Community Welfare in Nigeria (IICWIN) to open a STEM pathway for 20 underserved girls. This initiative aimed to empower the participants by providing foundational knowledge and practical skills in technology. For many of these girls, this program represented a significant milestone as it was their first time being exposed to STEM education, helping to broaden their horizons and inspire future career paths.
