Techbase Consultant Services was honored to participate as both a featured speaker and a primary sponsor at Rise Conference 1.0. Organised by the Healing Lives Initiative, this landmark event was specifically dedicated to empowering the next generation of leaders. The conference sought to challenge teenagers and youths to rise within their unique spheres of influence, providing them with the motivation and strategic insights needed to pursue excellence and refuse to settle for mediocrity in their personal and professional journeys.

![A young attendee taking notes at Rise Conference 1.0](photo:rise-conference-notes)

Founder and CEO Babatunde Awoyemi delivered an insightful talk at the conference, sharing his unique expertise and vision with the gathered audience. Techbase also served as a key sponsor for the event, further demonstrating its commitment to the industry. Additionally, the company was a featured participant in a book launch held during the same occasion, highlighting its support for new thought leadership.
