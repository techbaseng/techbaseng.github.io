![A student building a Scratch project on a laptop in a Lagos classroom](photo:bmhs-scratch)

Techbase has successfully launched an extensive coding and robotics training programme across the three prominent campuses of Baptist Model High Schools in Lagos—specifically Isolo, Ijegun, and Ikola. This new educational initiative is designed to provide students with practical, hands-on experience in software development and mechanical engineering, effectively preparing them for success in a technology-driven future.

![A Techbase instructor helping a pupil at a laptop](photo:bmhs-mentoring)

Throughout the course of the curriculum, students received comprehensive training in foundational Scratch Programming to grasp basic logic, followed by more advanced Python Programming for data handling and problem-solving. They also delved into modern web development using **HTML**, **CSS**, and **JavaScript** to build interactive and responsive user interfaces. Beyond coding, they learned to set up professional GitHub profiles for version control and created personal portfolio websites to showcase their growing bodies of work. This rigorous programme ran across multiple academic sessions, with students earning formal certificates of participation at the end of each term to recognize their progress and technical skill acquisition.
