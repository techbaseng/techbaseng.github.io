This structured training prepares students for various coding and robotics competitions held both online and in person. By participating in these events, students demonstrate the programming and technical problem-solving proficiency they have developed through the school’s partnership with Techbase.

Techbase has successfully introduced a comprehensive and structured coding and robotics training program to Knowledge Base International Schools. This curriculum is designed to grow with the students, featuring **Scratch Programming** to help younger learners grasp the fundamentals of logic and sequencing, while senior classes dive into **Python Programming, Web Development and AI App Development** to develop more advanced, industry-relevant technical skills.

![Pupils working at laptops in a Techbase coding class](photo:kbis-class-b)

Each session is delivered through a dedicated hands-on approach, ensuring personalised instruction, laptop by laptop, every single week, to foster true digital literacy and innovation.
