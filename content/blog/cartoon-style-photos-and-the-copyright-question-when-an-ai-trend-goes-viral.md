On 25 March 2025, OpenAI added a new image-generation ability to ChatGPT, and within days social media filled with photos restyled as hand-drawn cartoons, many imitating a famous animation studio's look. Demand was so high that the company said its systems were strained.

## Why it caught on

The tool follows detailed instructions, handles text inside images better than earlier ones and can edit a photo you upload. For many people it was the first time AI art felt personal and easy.

## The questions underneath

- **Style and copyright.** Can an AI copy the look of living artists or studios? Laws differ between countries and many cases are still before the courts.
- **Privacy.** Uploading a photo of yourself, and especially of children, shares personal data with a company.
- **Consent.** Editing someone else's photo without asking can embarrass or harm them.

## A simple family rule

Do not upload pictures of children to AI tools without thinking it through. Read how the service uses uploaded images, and keep a copy of the original.

## For young creators

Using AI is fine, but encourage children to draw, design and build things themselves first. A child who has made a character from scratch, even in Scratch, understands what goes into creative work and respects it.
