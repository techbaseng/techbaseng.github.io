This month we launched a new Techbase website, bringing our company pages and the Academy together in one place.

## What is new

- **A fresh design,** with a light and dark mode and pages that work on phones.
- **Our logo,** now across the site and in the browser tab.
- **Free accounts.** Create one to save your progress in the Academy and continue on any device.
- **Search everywhere.** Press Ctrl and K, or tap the search button, to find a lesson or page.
- **Portfolio and Team pages** that show the programmes we have run and the people behind them.
- **A blog archive** with a post for every month since January 2022, mixing our milestones with the big technology stories.
- **Works offline** for pages you have already visited.

## What has not changed

- **The lessons are still free.** All 472 lessons and projects across eight courses are open to learn from.
- **Our focus.** We still teach hands-on coding, robotics and AI to children, teachers and schools.

If something is broken or confusing, or you want to suggest a topic for the blog, [send us a message](../../contact/index.html). We read every one.

Thank you for learning with us.
