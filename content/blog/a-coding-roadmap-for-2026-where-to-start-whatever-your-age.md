Setting a learning goal is easier when you can see the road. Here is a simple map.

## Ages 8 to 12: play first

Start with block-based coding in **Scratch**: make a character move, tell a story, create a quiz. Aim for twenty minutes, two or three times a week. Our Scratch course has {n:techbase-scratch} free projects.

## Ages 10 to 14: build real things

Move to **HTML and CSS** to make web pages, then **Python** for logic and games. Add **Micro:bit** or robotics projects to see code control the physical world.

## Ages 14 to 18: build a portfolio

Learn **JavaScript** to make pages interactive, set up a **GitHub** profile and publish three projects. A portfolio shows more than a certificate.

## Adults and teachers

Pick one goal: build a simple website, automate a spreadsheet task or make an Android app. Learn by making that one thing.

## Habits that work at any age

1. **Little and often** beats one long weekend.
2. **Make something every week,** even tiny.
3. **Read error messages** calmly; they tell you where to look.
4. **Show your work** to someone.
