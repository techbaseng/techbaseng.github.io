With eight courses, it helps to have a map.

## "My child is under ten"
Start with **Scratch Programming**. Then try **Micro:bit Fundamentals** to make code control real things.

## "I want to build websites"
Follow the path **HTML Fundamentals**, then **CSS Styling**, then **JavaScript**. Each one builds on the previous.

## "I want to learn real programming"
Start with **Python Programming**. If you are new to coding, a few Scratch projects first will make it easier.

## "I want to make Android apps"
Do some **JavaScript** or **Python** first, then **Google Android Studio**.

## "I love robots"
Begin with **Micro:bit Fundamentals**, then move on to **Practical Robotics**.

## "I am a teacher"
Begin with **Scratch** or **HTML** so that you understand what pupils will experience. Then read our [guide to AI for lesson planning](../how-to-use-ai-to-create-better-lesson-plans-a-practical-guide-for-nigerian-teachers/index.html).

## Tips

- **Finish one course** before starting another.
- **Make notes** and small projects as you go.
- **Create a free account** to keep your place across devices.
