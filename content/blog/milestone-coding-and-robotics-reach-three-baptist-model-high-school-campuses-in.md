In August 2023 Techbase began a coding and robotics programme across the three campuses of Baptist Model High Schools in Lagos: Isolo, Ijegun and Ikola. It was our first programme to run across several campuses of one school group, and it let us design a curriculum that builds over several sessions.

## What students learned

- **Scratch** for the logic of programming.
- **Python** for data handling and problem solving.
- **HTML, CSS and JavaScript** to build interactive web pages.
- **GitHub** for saving and sharing work, with each student setting up a professional profile.
- **A personal portfolio website** to show what they had made.

## Certificates and portfolios

At the end of each term, students earned certificates of participation. More importantly, they finished with something they could show: a portfolio site and projects they had built themselves.

## Why it matters

A student who leaves school with a small body of real work stands out from one who has only attended lessons. It also builds the confidence to keep learning.
