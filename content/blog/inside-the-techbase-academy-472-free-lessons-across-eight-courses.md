The Techbase Academy has grown into a library of 472 free lessons and projects across eight courses. Here is what is in it.

## The eight courses

- **Scratch Programming** (35 projects): block coding for beginners.
- **HTML Fundamentals** (34 lessons): the structure of web pages.
- **CSS Styling** (73 lessons): colour, layout and responsive design.
- **JavaScript** (46 lessons): making pages interactive.
- **Python Programming** (45 lessons): logic, data and problem solving.
- **Google Android Studio** (72 lessons): building Android apps.
- **Micro:bit Fundamentals** (123 projects): code that controls lights, sensors and sound.
- **Practical Robotics** (44 projects): building and programming robots.

## How it works

Lessons are short and practical, with guided phases, practice tasks and mini projects. You can follow a course from start to finish, or dip into what you need. A free account saves your progress across devices.

## Who it is for

Children and teenagers, teachers who want to learn alongside their pupils, and adults starting a new skill. No prior experience is needed for the first courses of each path.

## Where to start

Open the [Academy](../../learn/index.html), choose a course and try the first lesson. If you are unsure, our [guide to choosing a course](../which-course-first-a-simple-map-of-the-techbase-academy/index.html) can help.
