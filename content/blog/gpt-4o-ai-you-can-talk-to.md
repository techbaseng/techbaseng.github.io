On 13 May 2024, OpenAI announced GPT-4o, where the "o" stands for "omni". It was designed to take in text, audio and images and respond with text, speech or images, with responses fast enough to feel like a conversation. Free users received access to many of its features.

## What is new

Earlier voice assistants converted speech to text, processed it and converted the answer back to speech, which added delays and lost detail such as tone. A single model that handles all of it directly can respond more quickly and more naturally.

## Useful ways to use it in learning

- **Practising a language** by talking and receiving gentle correction.
- **Accessibility.** Reading text aloud, describing images and helping people who find typing difficult.
- **Homework help** that explains step by step when you show it a problem.

## Cautions

- **It can still be wrong,** even when it sounds sure.
- **Privacy.** Do not share personal details or school records in a conversation.
- **Balance.** A friendly voice should never replace conversation with real people.

## For teachers

Try the tool yourself before recommending it. Ask it to explain a topic you know well and see where it makes mistakes. That experience is the best preparation for guiding pupils.
