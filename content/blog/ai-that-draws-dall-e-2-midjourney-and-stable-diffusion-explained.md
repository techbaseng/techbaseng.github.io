During 2022, three tools brought AI image creation to the public. DALL·E 2 from OpenAI was announced in April, Midjourney opened its public beta in July, and Stable Diffusion was released as open-source software on 22 August.

## How do they work?

These systems learn from huge collections of pictures paired with text descriptions. They discover patterns, for example what "a red bicycle against a blue wall" tends to look like, and then generate new images that match a written prompt. They do not copy-paste existing pictures, but they are shaped by the examples they have seen.

## Why it is exciting

Anyone can now sketch an idea in seconds, test different looks for a poster or illustrate a story without drawing skills. Teachers can create classroom visuals quickly.

## Why people are worried

- **Artists' work was used to train these systems,** often without permission or payment, and the courts and lawmakers are still working out what that means.
- **Fake images** can look convincing, so we must teach children to ask where a picture came from.
- **Bias.** The pictures reflect the data behind them, and that data does not represent everyone equally.

## Our approach

On this site, the photographs of our classes and events are always real ones. We use AI tools thoughtfully, and we teach learners to question what they see.
