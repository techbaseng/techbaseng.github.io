A little preparation makes the first weeks of term smoother. Use this checklist.

## Devices

- **Check batteries, chargers and screens.** Replace or repair what is broken.
- **Install updates** while you have a good connection.
- **Back up** projects and important files.

## Safety

- **Review passwords** and switch on two-step verification for key accounts.
- **Agree rules** for screen time and where devices are used.
- **Talk about AI use:** when it is allowed and how to say you used it.

## Routines

- **Set a regular slot** for practice, even twenty minutes, three times a week.
- **Keep a place** where devices are charged overnight, away from bedrooms.

## Learning goals

- **Choose one skill** for the term, such as a first Scratch game or a personal web page.
- **Decide how to show it,** for example a short demo for family.

## For teachers

- **Test the projector, laptops and internet** before the first lesson.
- **Prepare an offline plan** in case of a power or data failure.
- **Share a short guide** with parents about what pupils will do.
