On 30 November 2022, ChatGPT launched. Three years later, AI is built into search engines, phones, office software and homework apps. What has changed in schools?

## What has changed

- **Everyday use.** Many pupils and teachers use AI tools for explanations, drafting and planning, whether or not the school has a policy.
- **Teaching adapts.** More teachers use oral questions, in-class writing and project work to see what pupils really understand.
- **A new skill set.** Writing prompts, checking answers and citing sources are now taught in many places.

## What has not changed

- **Learning still needs effort.** A pupil who skips the struggle skips the learning.
- **Teachers matter most.** Tools help, but a teacher who knows the child makes the difference.
- **Access is uneven.** Devices, data and training are still scarce for many Nigerian pupils.

## What still needs doing

1. **Clear school policies** on how and when AI may be used.
2. **Teacher training** so that adults are confident, not afraid.
3. **Teaching the basics** of coding, maths and writing well.
4. **Open conversations with parents** about safe and honest use.
