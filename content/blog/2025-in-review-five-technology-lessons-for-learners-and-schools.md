Technology news moves fast, so December is a good time to ask what really mattered. These are five lessons from 2025 that we think will last.

## 1. AI is becoming ordinary
Reasoning models, voice assistants and agents moved from announcements to everyday tools. The question has shifted from "what is AI?" to "how should we use it well?"

## 2. Cheaper and open models widen access
Efficient and open models, including DeepSeek's release in January, showed that capable AI is not limited to a few firms. More people can build with it.

## 3. Fundamentals are more valuable, not less
As AI writes more code, people who understand logic, testing and design are the ones who can direct it and spot mistakes.

## 4. Safety is a habit
Updates, strong passwords, two-step verification and careful sharing of children's pictures prevent most problems.

## 5. Access decides who benefits
Devices, data and training are still uneven. Free, light, offline-friendly learning matters.

## Looking ahead

In 2026 we will keep doing what works: weekly hands-on classes, teacher training, free lessons and a close eye on new tools that really help. Thank you to every pupil, parent, teacher and school that learned alongside us this year.
