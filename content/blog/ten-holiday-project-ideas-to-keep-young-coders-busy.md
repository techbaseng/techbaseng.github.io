Long holidays can undo a term of progress, or they can be a time to build. Here are ten ideas.

## Beginners (Scratch)

1. **A quiz game** about your favourite subject, with a score.
2. **An animated story** about a day in your town.
3. **A catch-the-falling-objects game** with sound.

## Intermediate (HTML, CSS, Python)

4. **A personal web page** with a photo, a short biography and your favourite links.
5. **A tip calculator or currency converter** in Python.
6. **A guess-the-number game** that gives hints.
7. **A recipe or football-team page** with a nice layout.

## Challenge (JavaScript, robotics)

8. **A to-do list** that remembers items in the browser.
9. **A micro:bit step counter** or a simple alarm.
10. **A small robot** that avoids obstacles, built with a kit.

## Tips for success

- **Pick one project and finish it** before starting another.
- **Show it off** to family and friends.
- **Keep a notebook** of ideas and problems.
- **Use free lessons** if you get stuck.
